The paradigm

People genuinely believe their home is worth 30+ years of full‑time work, but a basic apartment‑sized room takes 150 hours of real labour and materials. Not $3 million. That gap — the difference between 150 hours and 30 years — is usury. Work is only fair if it’s evenly given back. We’re fixing that. ⚖️

I’m in the same boat as everyone else, & I’m not selling my life to a dead‑end job pretending I’m a “good contributor to society” while being siphoned dry. We’re not pawns. We’re not puppets. We’re living beings. 🧠

I’m done watching 13 billion get poured into new train stations no one uses when for that amount of money Victoria could solve homelessness 74x times. Or secure emergency housing for those in need AND homelessness 26 times over. 🚉

The reality

Governments only build stations instead of adding trains because adding trains = low cost, low political visibility, but Building stations = high cost, high political visibility. 🎭

But adding trains is all you need to curve a legitimate bottleneck in Melbourne.

Stations are: visible, symbolic, legacy projects — Ribbon‑cutting opportunities, “proof of investment”, politically defensible.

Increasing train frequency is: Invisible, technical, unglamorous, not headline‑worthy (to politicians). So governments choose the expensive visible option over the cheap functional option. 🔧

Melbourne’s crisis isn’t a resource problem — it’s a system design problem.
